# Compact room verification · v1.4.0

252 finite room components. The approved BMO geometry, materials, controls and portfolio content match `scripts/fixtures/approved-files.json`.

## Surface and placement corrections

The floor, rug, timber, book and clock artwork are mipmapped surface maps. Floating line layers, the rug's inverted hull, the separate floor light polygon and the procedural high-frequency paper noise are removed. Solid outlines weld coincident vertices before extrusion. Shade-band edges are antialiased.

All 16 room contact shadows use explicit floor/rug height, depth testing, no depth writes, a small depth bias and stable draw order. The BMO contact shadow follows the same floor offset. Twelve furniture feet retain exact floor/rug contact.

The eight tree trunks have 23 actual openings: bark triangles are cut away, rims follow the trunk curvature and bowls recess into the wood. The clock is mounted tangent to the bark with clearance; face artwork does not compete with the casing. Frames have supports and separate image planes. The player, radio and headset occupy distinct positions, and the headset rests on a stand. The book and shelf now have physical support.

## Compact interior and camera

The interior is 70 × 74 × 28 world units. All walls, floor, ceiling and windows remain real geometry. The camera orbit is capped at 27.5; projection near/far values are 0.2 and 160.

Occluding furniture never changes camera height. Collision uses a swept orbit path against actual solid mesh triangles and stops only direct travel into a surface. Its vertical component stays between the user's requested endpoints. A horizontal default circle changes height by less than 0.000001 world units.

## Rendering and verification

Canonical render: 398 draw calls and 351600 triangles, versus 686 and 603538 in v1.3.0. Rendering remains demand-driven. The fresh 4K render is actual 3840 × 2160 WebGL output.

Thirteen unit tests, syntax, model controls, screen alignment, responsive touch, external confirmations, resume PDF and WebGL fallback passed. Near/far room parallax remains genuine. `scripts/detail-check.mjs` checks resting-frame pixel equality, all shadow placements and the detail views. Hardware limitations are recorded in `ORBIT-REVIEW.md`.
